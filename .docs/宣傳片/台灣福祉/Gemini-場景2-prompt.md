---
用途: 給 Gemini 生成影片片段的 Prompt
對應: 分鏡稿 Scene 2 — 四大升級策略
旁白: "為了一次解決這些問題，台灣福祉決定把握政府輔導中小企業導入 AI 的契機。全公司中高階主管共同參與，從思維訓練開始，同步導入 ISO 標準化管理、整合正航與業務王系統、並導入 EEA AI 協作平台。"
時間: ~12 秒
---

## Prompt（複製到 Gemini）

```
Generate a 12-second cinematic corporate transformation video in dark navy and electric blue color scheme.

The visual narrative should show four key elements appearing one by one in a sleek, modern enterprise style:

1. **Mindset Training** — A glowing brain/thought icon with the text "思維訓練" (Mindset Training) fades in, surrounded by subtle neural network connections, symbolizing Minerva Thinking.

2. **ISO Standardization** — The scene transitions to show an ISO certification badge/shield icon with "ISO 導入" (ISO Implementation) appearing, representing standardized management.

3. **ERP & AI Integration** — A unified data flow emerges, showing old fragmented icons being replaced by a single integrated "EEA" system hub. Multiple disconnected data points converge into one clean, unified platform, representing the elimination of information silos, with text "ERP+AI 全面導入" (Full ERP & AI Implementation).

4. **AI Platform** — The final element is the "EEA" logo emerging at center, with "AI 協作平台" (AI Collaboration Platform) below it, radiating cyan/electric blue light lines outward, symbolizing the intelligent hub connecting everything.

The four elements should emerge sequentially with smooth transitions, building up to a final composition where all four elements coexist harmoniously.

Style requirements:
- Premium enterprise brand film aesthetic
- Dark navy (#0f172a) background with graphite accents
- Electric blue (#0066ff) and cyan (#00d4ff) glowing highlights
- Gold (#f59e0b) accents for transformation/highlight moments
- Clean, minimalist motion design — no cartoon, no gaming style
- Cinematic volumetric lighting
- Professional, sophisticated feel suitable for government presentation

At the bottom of the frame, a subtle subtitle line: "全公司中高階主管共同參與"
```

---

## 使用說明

1. 將上述 prompt 貼到 Gemini（或 Veo / Luma / Runway 等 AI 影片工具）
2. 產出約 12 秒片段
3. 搭配旁音軌一起剪入影片
