# EEA-CRM 資訊看板 — ChatGPT/DALL-E Prompt

請複製以下 prompt 到 ChatGPT，讓它生成 EEA-CRM 的概覽介紹圖。

---

## Prompt：EEA-CRM 智能客戶關係平台（英文，效果較好）

```
Create a professional enterprise CRM platform overview diagram in a dark navy and electric blue color scheme.

Title: "EEA-CRM 智能客戶關係平台 — AI-Powered Customer Relationship Management"

The diagram should show a central hub-and-spoke architecture:

=== CENTER (CORE) ===
EEA-CRM 智能中樞 (AI CRM Core)
- AI 意圖路由 (Intent Routing)
- 客戶 360° 視圖 (Customer 360)
- 智能體協作 (Agent Collaboration)

=== TOP SECTION (DATA LAYER) ===
標題: 客戶資料層 (Customer Data Layer)
- 客戶資料庫 (Customer DB) — 32K+ 客戶
- 聯絡人管理 (Contact Management)
- 標籤分類系統 (Tag Classification)
- 互動歷程 (Timeline) — 完整足跡

=== LEFT SECTION (AI CAPABILITIES) ===
標題: AI 智能層 (AI Intelligence)
- 客戶開發智能引擎 (Lead Generation AI)
- 銷售預測 (Sales Forecasting)
- 異常客戶偵測 (Churn Detection)
- 智能推薦 (Smart Recommendations)

=== RIGHT SECTION (BUSINESS TOOLS) ===
標題: 業務工具層 (Business Tools)
- 客戶地圖全覽 (Customer Map) — 地理分布可視化
- 經營績效看板 (Performance Dashboard) — KPI/區域/部門
- 業務員排行 (Sales Ranking Top 10)
- 業績趨勢分析 (Revenue Trend)

=== BOTTOM SECTION (COMMUNICATION) ===
標題: 客戶互動層 (Customer Engagement)
- LINE 業務幫手 (LINE Business Assistant)
- 潛在客戶開發 (Lead Development)
- 人情關懷服務 (Relationship Care)
- 即時通報 (Instant Notifications)

=== CONNECTING ELEMENTS ===
- Data flows represented by glowing cyan/electric blue lines connecting all layers
- AI icons (sparkle/neural) placed at intersection points showing AI augmentation at each layer
- A vertical "安全邊界 (Security Boundary)" bar on the right side

=== VISUAL STYLE ===
- Dark navy gradient background (#0f172a to #1e3a5f)
- Electric blue (#0066ff) and cyan (#00d4ff) accents
- Gold (#f59e0b) highlights for business value metrics
- Clean minimalist card/module shapes with subtle glow effects
- Professional enterprise infographic style
- No cartoon, no gaming, no crypto aesthetics

Bottom tagline: "從客戶資料到業務洞察，AI 驅動的智能客戶關係管理"
```

## 使用場景

這張圖可以用在：
1. **EEA AIBox 總覽頁**的 CRM 模組介紹區塊
2. **業務拓展中心**頁面的 Header 區
3. 明天視察時作為 CRM 模組的投影片素材
